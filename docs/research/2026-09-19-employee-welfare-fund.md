# กองทุนสงเคราะห์ลูกจ้าง (Employee Welfare Fund, EWF) — what starts 1 Oct 2026

Researched 2026-09-19 (multi-agent sweep, 40 claims adversarially fact-checked;
37 CONFIRMED, 3 REFUTED-and-corrected). Primary sources: ราชกิจจานุเบกษา,
กรมสวัสดิการและคุ้มครองแรงงาน (กสร./DLPW) คำชี้แจง, มาตรา 126–138
พ.ร.บ.คุ้มครองแรงงาน พ.ศ. 2541.

## 2. Rates and mechanics

| | Employee (เงินสะสม) | Employer (เงินสมทบ) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Oct 2026 – 30 Sep 2031 | **0.25% of ค่าจ้าง** | **0.25% of ค่าจ้าง** |
| From 1 Oct 2031 | 0.50% | 0.50% |

- **Base = ค่าจ้าง, with NO ceiling** — unlike ประกันสังคม's ฿15,000 cap. มาตรา 131
  caps the *rate* at 5% of wages, not the wage base.
- **First deduction = October 2026 wages. First remittance + first สกล.3 filing
  due 15 November 2026.** Thereafter the 15th of the following month.
- Late/short remittance: **เงินเพิ่ม 5% per month** (มาตรา 131). Failing to file
  the employee roster is criminal under มาตรา 156 (≤6 months / ฿10,000).
- Employers who already file สปส.1-01 / สปส.1-03 / สปส.6-15 with SSO are *deemed*
  to have filed สกล.3 (มาตรา 130 วรรคแปด) — but the new e-Service ประกาศ implies
  portal registration regardless. **Verify with the provincial office.**

## 3. Our in-house เงินสะสม 5% does NOT exempt us

Exemption (มาตรา 130 วรรคสอง) has exactly two routes:

1. A **registered กองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ (PVD)** under พ.ร.บ.กองทุนสำรองเลี้ยงชีพ 2530 —
   and only for the employees actually enrolled. Probationers / opt-outs still
   join the EWF.
2. An **in-house scheme meeting กฎกระทรวงฯ พ.ศ. 2567** cumulatively:
   written scheme document; employer **matching เงินสมทบ**; combined rate **not
   below the PVD floor (2%–15% of wages)**; the money **deposited at a commercial
   bank in a deposit account in each employee's own name**; account details
   notified within 7 days; inspection answered within 15 days; passbook +
   termination letter returned within 30 days of separation.

Our scheme is a book-entry balance on the hotel's own books, with no employer
match and no per-employee bank account. It fails at least three conditions.
**We are in the EWF** (subject to §5 below).

Corollary: the "make our own scheme the exemption" path would cost **≥2% of
payroll in employer matching** versus **0.25%** for simply joining. Joining is
an order of magnitude cheaper.

## 4. The conversion question — what is settled and what is not

**Settled:** the employer's 0.25% เงินสมทบ is **new money**. There is no
mechanism to fund it from the employee's deduction (มาตรา 131 วรรคสอง/สาม bars
the employer from pleading a failure to deduct as a reason not to remit). The
hotel's cost rises by 0.25% of payroll no matter what we do with the in-house 5%.

**Not settled by the EWF law:** whether the *employee's* 0.25% comes out of the
existing 5% (→ 4.75% in-house + 0.25% EWF, take-home unchanged) or on top of it
(→ 5% + 0.25%, take-home drops ~0.25%). That is a **contract / สภาพการจ้าง**
question. Reducing an existing welfare benefit unilaterally can be a
การเปลี่ยนแปลงสภาพการจ้างที่ไม่เป็นคุณแก่ลูกจ้าง requiring employee consent —
the simple mechanism is an individual **หนังสือยินยอม** plus a ประกาศ, signed
*before* the October payroll is run.

**Timing answer: the switch lands on the 2026-10 cycle — not earlier.**
EWF attaches to wages from 1 Oct 2026, so the September cycle (paid 5 Oct) is
unaffected. The October cycle is prepared end-October, paid 5 Nov, remitted by
15 Nov. Doing the carve-out in any earlier cycle under-funds the in-house
savings with nothing to remit against; doing it later means deducting 5% in
October and reconciling backwards.

## 5. Open questions the owner must answer (not researchable)

1. **Is HF and HF Ville one employer or two?** มาตรา 130 says *กิจการ* with ten
   or more employees; the DLPW คำชี้แจง glosses it as สถานประกอบกิจการ. One
   worksheet carries ~15 staff across both properties. If they are two separate
   registered employers each under 10, neither is covered. **This decides
   whether we contribute at all.**
2. **Service charge in the base?** The single biggest hotel-specific question.
   Secondary sources exclude it; ฎีกา on service charge as ค่าจ้าง is not
   uniform; no DLPW statement found. A 5%/month surcharge attaches to
   under-remittance, so confirm with the labour welfare office.
3. **OT in the base?** มาตรา 5 defines ค่าจ้าง as remuneration for *normal*
   working time, and ค่าล่วงเวลา is a separate defined term → excluded. Thai HR
   vendors contradict each other (Dharmniti excludes, empeo includes). Our
   worksheet keeps OT in a separate `ot` column, so excluding it is the default
   and matches the statute.
4. **Rounding rule** — no source states one. At 0.25% satang will occur
   (e.g. ฿12,450 → ฿31.125). We round to 2 dp like every other column until DLPW
   says otherwise.
5. **Tax treatment** — no Revenue Code provision found giving EWF the PVD-style
   deduction. Ask the accountant.
6. **Whether ours is documented as ข้อบังคับเกี่ยวกับการทำงาน or individual
   หนังสือยินยอม** — decides how hard a 5% → 4.75% change is.

## 6. Cost

At ~15 staff, 0.25% of monthly base payroll is roughly **฿400–450/month per
side** — about ฿5k/year of new employer cost. The decision is about staff
communication and correctness, not about the money.
